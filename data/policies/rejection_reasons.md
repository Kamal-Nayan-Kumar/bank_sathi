# Why a card was not recommended

When a card is not in your list, it is because at least one published
requirement was not met. We tell you which one and how far off it was, because
"you don't qualify" on its own tells you nothing you can act on.

## Every card is checked on its own terms

We assess each card against your profile separately. You may be perfectly
eligible for one card and not for another, and being turned down for a premium
card says nothing about the entry cards.

You are told about every requirement a card failed, not just the first. Fixing
one blocker usually reveals the next, and finding out one at a time is a waste
of your time.

## The requirements a card has

Every card in our catalogue publishes the same set of things:

- **Minimum monthly income.** What we need your income to be, compared against
  yours.
- **Minimum credit score.** Cards we cannot assess at all on your file are not
  shown as near misses.
- **Age window.** Most cards have both a floor and a ceiling.
- **Employment type.** Some cards are limited to particular applicant types.
- **Whether it is open.** A withdrawn card is never recommended.

## Reading a near miss

A near miss is a card you failed by a small margin on a single requirement.
We mark those separately and tell you the exact distance: how much more income a
month, or how many credit-score points.

A near miss is not a promise that you will qualify. It is a statement that the
gap is small enough to be worth naming.

## What we will not suggest

We do not advise taking on a loan, applying for more cards, or otherwise
increasing your obligations in order to move a score. Those actions generally
work against you, and this page exists to give you advice that does not.

Closing a card you use regularly is also unhelpful: it shortens your credit
history, which is part of what a score rewards.

## What actually helps

- Autopay on every card, for at least the minimum due amount.
- Clearing the statement balance in full each month.
- Space applications a few months apart.
- One card, kept in good standing, for a year.
