# How we value a card

Every card we recommend has been reduced to one number: what it is worth to
you over a year, given how you actually spend.

## The idea

Rewards are only worth something in relation to your own spending. A card that
pays well on fuel is worthless to someone who never fills a petrol tank, and one
that pays a flat rate on everything can beat a headline rate that applies to a
category you do not use.

So each card is measured against your monthly spend, category by category.

## What goes into the number

- **Reward value.** What you would actually earn, with monthly caps respected.
  A generous rate behind a low cap is not a generous rate.
- **Lounge access.** Counted only if you told us you want travel or lounge
  benefits. We do not credit a benefit you will not use.
- **The annual fee, if you would actually pay it.** Many cards waive it against
  annual spend. If your spend meets the published threshold, we score the fee as
  zero rather than pretending it is a small number.

A card whose value does not clear the fee is not recommended at all, however
good it looks on paper.

## How the order is decided

Four things, weighted:

1. **Net value**, which is the number above. It dominates, because rupees are
   the point.
2. **How well the card rewards where you actually spend.** Weighted by your
   spending, not by how many categories the card happens to cover.
3. **Whether it matches what you asked for**, if you said. If you did not
   express a preference, this neither helps nor hurts you.
4. **Whether the fee is comfortable** against what you get back.

The weights are applied in the same order every time, and the breakdown for each
card is shown in the interface. If two cards are ordered differently than you
expected, the numbers behind that ordering are on screen.

## Limits are estimates

The indicative credit limit we show is your income multiplied by a factor that
depends on the card tier. It exists so you can compare cards against each other.

It is not a sanctioned limit and it is not an offer.
